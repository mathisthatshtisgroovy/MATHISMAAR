abstract: >-
  BORDERS OF NEUTRALITY is a multimedia installation that reads the Alps as a 
  shifting border archive, where melting glaciers redraw the lines of Swiss 
  neutrality. A staged view from a mountain bench fractures under sonic 
  disruptions and visual glitches. The project traces how geological 
  aesthetics are co-opted into policy as visual tools of authority.
